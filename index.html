Tournament Rules & Tiebreaker Hierarchy

Draft order for the upcoming fantasy season is bound strictly to your assigned nation's live performance in the World Cup. Ranks adjust automatically every 6 hours based on the latest official statistics.

1. Progress Stage Teams further along in the knockout rounds outrank all teams eliminated in previous stages.
2. Match Wins If nations exit during the exact same round, the manager with more total match wins takes priority.
3. Goal Difference If stage and wins are identical, placement drops down to the superior overall tournament Goal Difference (GD).
4. Pre-Tournament Odds Absolute tiebreaker: The country with the lower pre-kickoff betting odds wins the position (underdog advantage).

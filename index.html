Tournament Rules & Tiebreaker Hierarchy

Draft order is bound to your assigned nation's live performance. Ranks calculate every 60 minutes.

1. Progress StageTeams in further knockout brackets completely outrank previously eliminated squads.
2. Match WinsIf teams drop out in the same global stage, total absolute match wins decide the position.
3. Goal DifferenceIf wins are matched, priorities fall directly onto the total tournament Goal Difference (GD).
4. Entry OddsAbsolute tiebreaker: The country carrying lower baseline pre-kickoff betting values secures the pick.

Column Key Definitions

W/P: Wins / Matches Played
GD: Goal Difference (F = Goals For, A = Goals Against)
Odds: Pre-tournament baseline bookmaker value
